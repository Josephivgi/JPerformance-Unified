JPerformance Unified is one app for this PC and your Linux servers. It replaces JPerformance and JServer.

**New**

- One Overview for every machine: CPU over the last hour, memory, the fullest disk, network, and what uses the most memory on each.
- Findings for all machines in one list, worst first, with a plain explanation of what happened and when.
- This PC has six tabs: Now, History, Apps, Running, Recordings and Insights.
- History charts follow one pointer. Hover to see the same moment on every chart and which apps were behind it. Click to keep that moment open.
- A weekday by hour heatmap shows when this PC, or one app, is busiest.
- Servers over SSH are optional and off until you turn them on in Settings. You pick which hosts from your SSH config to watch.
- Each server shows where its memory goes (programs, ZFS cache, file cache), processes grouped by VM, container or service, its disks, history and findings.
- A server that cannot be reached shows why and its last history. It is not retried on its own, so fail2ban will not ban you. Press Retry when it is back.
- An SSH keys page checks your keys and SSH config.
- Light, dark and system themes in Settings > Appearance.

**Improved**

- Your history from JPerformance and JServer is copied on first start. The old apps and their data are not changed.
- One background recorder now records this PC and your servers, even with the window closed.
